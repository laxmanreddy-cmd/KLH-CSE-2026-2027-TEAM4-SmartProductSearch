CO5

NP / NP-Hard Optimisation Lab

Understand P, NP, NP-Complete, NP-Hard. Watch exact vs approximate solution behaviour.

P
Solvable in polynomial time. e.g. Sorting, Shortest Path, Max Flow.
NP
Solution verifiable in polynomial time. Includes all of P. e.g. Subset Sum.
NP-Complete
Hardest problems in NP. Every NP problem reduces to these. e.g. 3-SAT, Vertex Cover, Independent Set.
NP-Hard
At least as hard as NP-Complete. May not even be in NP. e.g. TSP optimisation, Halting Problem.
